# Original images

Files `boat1.jpg`–`boat4.jpg` are the OpenCV extra stitching test photographs
of the Neva waterfront / Peter and Paul Fortress (Saint Petersburg).

Source:
https://github.com/opencv/opencv_extra/tree/4.x/testdata/stitching

They are used here because they are publicly available, already form a
genuine multi-view sequence of one scene, and contain distinctive
structure (ship rigging, cathedral spire, facades, trees) plus some
difficult regions (water, sky). No pixel values were invented.

- `boat1.jpg`, `boat2.jpg`, `boat3.jpg` — primary three-image panorama
- `boat4.jpg` — wider baseline for the viewpoint experiment
